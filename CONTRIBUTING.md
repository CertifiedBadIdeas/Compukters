GitHub's inbound=outbound contribution terms.

You retain ownership of your contribution. By submitting it, you confirm that
you have the authority to license it on these terms. A separate contributor
license agreement does not currently apply.

Do not copy third-party code, assets, generated output, or other material into
a contribution unless its license is compatible and its origin, copyright
notices, and complete license text are included. Describe such material in the
pull request and update `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` when it will be distributed.

## Project decisions

The maintainer has final authority over the official repositories, roadmap,
architecture, feature set, accepted contributions, and releases. Acceptance of
a contribution does not grant governance, roadmap, release, or veto rights.
The maintainer may modify, replace, or remove accepted work as the project
changes direction.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused, link the Roadmap issue which owns the work, and list the
verification commands you ran. User-visible Minecraft or UI changes should
include a screenshot or short recording when practical.
