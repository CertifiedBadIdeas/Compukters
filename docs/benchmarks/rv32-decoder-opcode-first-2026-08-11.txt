The automatic gate required at least four wins in five processes and a median
improvement of at least 2% in both scenarios. Opcode-first won every process in
both scenarios, but the median effect was smaller than 2%, so the script exited
1 and reported rejected. The project owner explicitly retained opcode-first:
the direction was consistent in all ten paired process outcomes, semantics and
resources were unchanged, and the opcode-first structure does not carry a
maintenance penalty over eager extraction.
