registration is claimed.

including reviews, screenshots, links, unmodified modpacks, and accurate
compatibility statements. You may also describe a derivative as a “fork of
Compukters”.

A fork or independently maintained distribution must use a distinct primary
name and logo, and must clearly state that it is unofficial. It must not imply
that Compukters produced, sponsors, endorses, or maintains it.

origin of a project. Uses independently permitted by applicable law are not
restricted by this policy.
